# Ask to be exempted from battery optimisation (Samsung S25)

Phase 1 ([plan](https://github.com/bflutz/nytka-server/blob/phase1/docs/voice-recorder-plan.md), R7). The app doesn't request `REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS` (`docs/explanation/link-drops.md:56-57`). On Samsung, background limits stop long recordings. PhoneMicService already runs as a microphone foreground service.

**Change:**
- Declare `REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS` in the oss flavor (and in play only if the policy allows; say which).
- When the phone-mic switch is first turned on and `PowerManager.isIgnoringBatteryOptimizations` is false, show a plain screen explaining why, then open `ACTION_REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS`.
- Add a status row ('Battery: unrestricted / restricted', with a fix button) and a short Samsung tip: Settings > Apps > Nytka > Battery > Unrestricted, and keep it out of 'Sleeping apps'.
- Update link-drops.md.

**Done when:**
- The prompt appears once per denial and never blocks recording.
- The status row reflects the real state, with a unit test of the state logic.
- The oss build, unit tests and ktlint pass.
